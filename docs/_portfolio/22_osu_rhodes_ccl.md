Rhodes Hall's Critical Care Laboratory operates 24/7 to provide blood testing to the entire Wexner Medical Center system. This expansion was timed with the University Hospital ("[Tower](https://wexnermedical.osu.edu/lp/new-inpatient-hospital-tower)") construction to ensure adequate testing capacity. The expansion was built with the lab in continuous operation!

Though laboratory equipment is relatively low power, most is receptacle connected, finding panels with available capacity and circuits was a challenge. This was compounded by coordination with user groups to ensure equipment was powered from the appropriate branch of emergency power (life-safety, critical, equipment or UPS branches). We also coordinated with representatives of Siemens, Beckman Coulter and Swisslog to provide electrical/technology infrastructure for their equipment.

The complexity of documentation and coordination forced me to develop organizational systems to track loads, power source, voltage, telecomm requirements and equipment locations. On no sunsequent project has tracking equipment ever felt as difficult.
