It's exciting to help improve [accessibility in Ohio](https://www.statenews.org/section/the-ohio-newsroom/2025-04-04/every-human-needs-a-restroom-ohioans-with-disabilities-advocate-for-adult-sized-changing-tables). Though this project was uncomplicated from an engineering perspective, there's an eficiency I'm quite proud of. Original project scope included a new panelboard. However, when examining field visit photos, I noticed that the desired capacity could be achieved by upsizing the feeder and overcurrent protection. This was a direct cost-savings to the owner.
