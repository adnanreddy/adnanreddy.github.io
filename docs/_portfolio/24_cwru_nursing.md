This project was one of the first tests of the lighting procedure I developed. By making better use of Revit and pyRevit, we were able to standardize and streamline how we handle calculation of lighting loads and lighting fixture schedules. The new procedure came at the perfect time, as we integrated lighting design from Lam Partners Inc into our model.

I also wrote detailed notes for the make-safe phase of this project, to ensure that a TDR serving multiple buildings on CWRU's campus was powered through the demolition with minimal downtime.
